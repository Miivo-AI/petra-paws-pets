# WhatsApp Booking Confirmations — What This Means For Your Phone

Please read this before we connect your WhatsApp number. Connecting
changes how the WhatsApp Business app works on your phone, and some of
those changes cannot be undone. Nothing happens until you sit down with
us and complete the connection together.

## What we're setting up

Right now, every customer who books gets a confirmation email. We're
adding a WhatsApp confirmation on top of that, sent automatically the
moment a booking is confirmed, so customers get the details where they
actually read messages.

The important part: this uses **your existing business number**. You keep
chatting to customers from the WhatsApp Business app on your phone
exactly as you do today, and the website sends its confirmations from the
same number. Customers see one number, not two.

## What you need before we start

- **A payment method on the WhatsApp account.** Messages you type
  yourself on your phone stay free. Messages the website sends are
  charged by Meta at a few fils each. Without a payment method attached,
  the automated confirmations simply won't send.
- **Your phone, charged and with you.** The connection is done from the
  phone, and there's a time limit once we begin (see below).
- **About an hour set aside.** Most of it is waiting, but we shouldn't
  rush it.

## The permanent changes to your WhatsApp Business app

These take effect the moment we connect, and they are not reversible
while the number stays connected:

- **Devices get logged out.** If you use WhatsApp Business on a computer
  or tablet as well as your phone, those will be logged out and you'll
  need to pair them again afterwards.
- **Broadcast lists stop working for new messages.** Existing lists
  become read-only, and you won't be able to create new ones. If you
  currently use broadcast lists to send offers, tell us now and we'll
  plan around it.
- **Some chat features switch off** for all your one-to-one chats:
  disappearing messages, view-once photos, and live location sharing.
- **Group chats stay private to your phone.** They never reach the
  website, and the website can never see or send to them.
- **WhatsApp for Windows and WearOS won't work** as companion devices.

If any of these are part of how you run the business day to day, say so
before we connect rather than after.

## Three rules to follow afterwards, permanently

Breaking any of these disconnects the number, and reconnecting means
starting the whole process again.

1. **Don't uninstall the WhatsApp Business app.** Uninstalling
   disconnects the account.
2. **Open the app at least once every 10 days.** Meta disconnects numbers
   after roughly two weeks with no activity on the main phone. Ten days
   gives you a safe margin. Just opening it is enough.
3. **Never register that number on regular (personal) WhatsApp.** That
   disconnects it too.

## The connection appointment

Once we begin, Meta gives us exactly **24 hours** to copy your contacts
and recent chat history across, and exactly **one attempt at each**. The
website starts both automatically the moment you finish, and shows how
much time is left.

What we need from you during this: **keep the WhatsApp Business app open
on your phone** while it runs. If a step fails there's a "Retry sync"
button, but if the 24 hours run out we have to disconnect and start over.

## What your customers will see

The confirmation message reads roughly like this:

> Hi Sarah, your Petra Paws booking PP-1234 is confirmed for 15 Aug 2026
> at 10:00 AM (Full Grooming for Bella). We'll contact you shortly to
> confirm the details. Reply STOP to stop WhatsApp updates.

Two things worth knowing:

- **Customers choose whether to get these.** There's a tick box under the
  phone number on the booking form. It's ticked by default, but if a
  customer unticks it, they never receive a WhatsApp message from the
  site.
- **STOP works, and we honour it.** If a customer replies STOP, the site
  stops messaging them automatically and permanently, even if they book
  again later. They can reply START to turn it back on. Meta requires
  this, and ignoring it puts the number at risk.

We deliberately did **not** treat "CANCEL" as an opt-out word, because
for a grooming business that means "cancel my appointment" — so if a
customer replies CANCEL, that's a message for you to read and act on, not
an unsubscribe.

Email confirmations keep going out to everyone regardless, so no customer
is ever left without their booking details.

## A limit worth knowing about early

Until Meta finishes verifying the business, the number can only message
**250 different people per 24 hours**. That's well above current booking
volumes, so it shouldn't bite, but it's the reason we push to get
verification done rather than leaving it.

## If something looks wrong

There's a WhatsApp page in your admin area that shows whether the
connection is healthy, whether Meta still considers the number connected,
your message quality rating, and the last few messages that failed. It
also has a **Send a test message** button that messages your own number,
which is the fastest way to check things still work.

If confirmations stop arriving, check that page first and send us a
screenshot. In almost every case the cause is one of the three rules
above.
